## Editorial cautions

- Preserve the exact financial year and date in every question.
- For policy targets and budget estimates, phrase the question as a reported/proposed/estimated figure, not as an achieved outcome.
- CA01 and CA02 are separate figures from the same official address; avoid asking them as near-duplicate questions in one paper.
- Recheck each source immediately before publishing a paper. If a source is revised or a later official update supersedes it, update the question and its `asOfDate`.
- The final question records must include `source` (a URL) and `asOfDate` (ISO date, e.g. `2026-11-13`).
